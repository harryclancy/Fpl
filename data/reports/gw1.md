## Big picture

Arsenal enter the season as defending champions after ending a 22-year title drought, and open as bookmaker favourites again (approx. +125, approx. 44% implied win probability per one model), with Man City (approx. +333, approx. 20%), Liverpool (approx. 13%), Chelsea (approx. 12%) and Man Utd (approx. 10%) as the chasing pack. Arsenal reportedly beat City comfortably in the Community Shield curtain-raiser — an early form signal, not a fixture result.

An early GW1 "template" is already forming from aggregated manager data (2,000+ squads sampled by one tracker): **Haaland, Bruno Fernandes, João Pedro, Szoboszlai and Rogers** are the most-selected names, with Raya the only defender above 30% ownership. Analysts caution this early ownership is inflated by managers who registered a squad without refining it yet — treat it as a signal, not gospel, until closer to deadline.

## Captaincy consensus

- **Top pick: Haaland** (Man City, home vs Bournemouth) — highest-projected player on the board, already the most-owned player in the pool at roughly 71% (Fantasy Football Scout). Historically FPL's best starter: Haaland has been the top scorer after the first 6 gameweeks in all four of his City seasons, averaging 9 goals and 61.3 points over that stretch.
- **Alternative: Bruno Fernandes** (Man Utd, away at Hull City) — highest-projected midfielder most weeks. Man Utd have arguably the best opening schedule of any side, with back-to-back fixtures against promoted Hull City and Ipswich Town.
- **Differential: Bukayo Saka** (Arsenal, home vs Coventry) — Arsenal have the softest fixture on the opening slate, and Saka's captaincy ownership sits around just 10%, making him a genuine differential armband if you want to break from the Haaland/Bruno consensus.
- **Differential: Gabriel** (Arsenal) — set-piece and clean-sheet upside; mentioned repeatedly across FPL content as a "brave" differential captain given Arsenal's fixture and defensive solidity.

## Player notes (what FPL managers and analysts are actually saying)

- **Haaland** (Man City, £14.5m-ish) — The Scout's #1 "must-have" for the season. 71% ownership already. Historically explosive starts: top scorer after the first 6 GWs in all 4 of his City seasons (9 goals, 61.3 pts average over that stretch). Home vs Bournemouth is a favourable curtain-raiser.
- **Bruno Fernandes** (Man Utd) — Also one of The Scout's must-haves. United's fixture swing (Hull away, then Ipswich at home) is repeatedly cited across FPL content as the best two-gameweek run of any side, with pundits saying they'll be "tripling up" on United and Arsenal attackers against the promoted sides.
- **João Pedro** (Chelsea, ~£7.5m) — Widely flagged as the value pick of the gameweek: 15 goals + 9 assists last season at that price point. He was left out of Brazil's World Cup squad, which freed him up for a full pre-season under Xabi Alonso — he scored 8 goals in pre-season, more than any other FPL-relevant player. Consensus explicitly says: start him, but don't captain him — Haaland or Bruno should still hold the armband in a squad that has either.
- **Gabriel** (Arsenal) — One of The Scout's must-haves; repeatedly framed as a genuine differential captain option on set-piece and clean-sheet threat, not just a defensive holding pick.
- **Szoboszlai & Rogers** — Both showing up in the earliest cross-manager "template" data alongside Haaland/Bruno/João Pedro — early consensus picks, though analysts note this could shift as managers finalise squads closer to deadline.
- **Luke Shaw** (Man Utd, £4.5m) — The standout budget defender: most-owned £4.5m defender (~24%) and explicitly favoured by "elite" FPL managers in tracked data. Benefits from the same Hull-then-Ipswich fixture swing as Bruno.
- **Leif Davis** (Ipswich, £4.0m) — Called out for elite underlying numbers at the very cheap end: 8.90 xA last season, the highest of any Championship defender, and he takes Ipswich's set pieces — bonus-point and assist equity most weeks he starts.
- **Ferdi Kadioglu** (Brighton) — Flagged as under-the-radar: just ~3.1% ownership despite 3.2 pts/game over 3,000+ minutes and 34 starts last season. Described as "about as nailed as it gets" for a name nobody's talking about yet.
- **Kai Havertz** — The headline GW1 differential pick across multiple sites: sub-7% ownership with top-tier underlying expected-goals/assists numbers.
- **Jean-Philippe Mateta** — Flagged as a season-long (not just GW1) differential on strong underlying attacking numbers and a reasonable fixture.
- **Tarkowski, Maguire, Tavernier** — All mentioned as overlooked defensive differentials with strong underlying defensive numbers, worth a look if you want to break from the Shaw/Raya template at the back.
- **Ngumoha** (Liverpool) — Described as the highest-variance flier of the gameweek: very low ownership, boom-or-bust — sites suggest treating it as a punt with an exit plan if he doesn't start, not a set-and-forget pick.

## Fixture notes

- Arsenal host Coventry (promoted) at the Emirates — strong fixture for Arsenal attackers and clean-sheet assets.
- Man City host Bournemouth — Haaland's headline fixture.
- Man Utd away at Hull City, then home to Ipswich Town — widely cited as the best two-gameweek fixture swing in the league, driving both the Bruno captaincy case and the Luke Shaw budget-defender case.
